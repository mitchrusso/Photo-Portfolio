## Create and activate more than one website

To switch between websites, select **My Website** in the dashboard menu, then choose the website you want to edit from the dropdown. PhotoView opens that website's builder and keeps its name visible in the top toolbar so you always know which website you are changing.

1. Open **Settings**, then **My Websites**.
2. Enter a recognizable name and choose **Create free draft**. You can create unlimited drafts. Drafts have no public URL and do not create a charge.
3. Choose **Edit website**, select the portfolios and catalog content for that audience, then build, save, and preview the draft.
4. Return to **Settings → My Websites**. Under **Activate this website**, enter the PhotoView.io address you want.
5. On a regular paid account, choose **Activate for $1.29/month**. The add-on follows the account's existing monthly or annual billing cycle and is consolidated onto its next renewal without a separate prorated charge today.
6. On a lifetime account, choose either **$1.29 monthly** or the displayed prorated annual balance. The annual option covers the time remaining until the account anniversary and then renews at $15.48 per year.
7. Complete the paid commitment. PhotoView assigns the public URL only after activation succeeds.
8. To use your own domain, open the activated website and choose **Address**. The subscriber supplies and pays for the domain; the included PhotoView.io address remains available at no extra charge.

Deleting an additional website stops its public access immediately and removes it from the next renewal. Website activation charges are separate from photo and video storage charges.

Each lesson includes a specific outcome, numbered steps, a current PhotoView screenshot, a screenshot caption, and previous/next navigation.

For any standard or custom website page with **Show navigation link** enabled, **Link position** offers **Show at top**, **Show at bottom**, or **Show at top and bottom**. The third option places the same page link in both the website header and footer in Live Canvas, Preview, and the published website.

The About page can use a still photograph, a visitor-controlled introduction video, or text alone. About video accepts MP4 or MOV up to 200 MB and 90 seconds. When both photo and video are saved, the video is displayed and the photo serves as its poster and fallback.

## Help inside the product

- **Ask AI How To** answers questions about PhotoView controls and workflows.
- **Edit Hints** identifies the setting behind the part of the website being edited.
- **Tours** guides a subscriber through a verified multi-step task.
- **Help Center** provides complete illustrated lessons that can be followed at the subscriber's own pace.

## Editorial standard for future tutorials

Every new tutorial should:

- name the result the subscriber will achieve;
- use the exact labels found in the current interface;
- keep steps short and task-oriented;
- include a current screenshot when the visual location matters;
- describe the difference between saving, previewing, and publishing;
- cover desktop and mobile behavior when they differ;
- avoid claiming a draft or change is public until publication is verified.
