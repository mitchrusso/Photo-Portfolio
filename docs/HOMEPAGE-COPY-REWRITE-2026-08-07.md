## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is PhotoView a photo editor?

No. Finish your photographs in Lightroom, Capture One, Photoshop, Affinity, DxO, ON1, or the editor you already use. PhotoView is where you store, curate, present, publish, and share the finished work.

### Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Publish immediately with a personal PhotoView.io address, then connect a domain you already own. Supported providers offer one-click setup, with guided DNS instructions available everywhere else.

### Can I add PhotoView to my current website?

Yes. Embed one portfolio or your complete portfolio grid in an existing website, blog, or Shopify page. Multiple named embed profiles let you use different work in different places.

### Will my photographs look good on a phone?

Yes. PhotoView uses responsive display files and full-screen mobile viewing designed specifically for portrait, landscape, square, and panoramic photographs.

### Can I keep a portfolio private?

Yes. Control visibility by Gallery or Portfolio, share private links, add password protection, and optionally require email two-factor verification.

### Can I import directly from Lightroom Classic?

Yes. The PhotoView Lightroom Plugin sends finished rendered photographs—up to 50 MB each—to a new or existing portfolio without resizing or cropping them.

### Does PhotoView support video?

Yes. Combine photographs with MP4 or MOV video in portfolios, use a looping Hero video, or introduce yourself with video on the About page.

### What happens if I change templates?

Your portfolios and website content remain available. The new template changes the presentation rather than forcing you to rebuild the site from the beginning.

### Is PhotoView built for professional photographers?

PhotoView is built first for passionate photographers who care deeply about presentation. Wedding, product, commercial, fine-art, and editorial photographers can also use its custom pages, focused portfolios, embeds, private links, contact forms, and custom domains to present work professionally.
